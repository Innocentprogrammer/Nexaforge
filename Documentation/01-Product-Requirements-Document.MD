# 1. Executive Summary

NexaInvite is an AI-powered wedding invitation and event management platform that enables users to create, customize, share, and manage wedding invitations without requiring graphic design skills.

Unlike traditional invitation websites that focus solely on card creation, NexaInvite provides a complete digital wedding experience by combining invitation design, RSVP management, guest tracking, event websites, and vendor collaboration into one integrated platform.

The product is designed to serve both individual customers and business partners such as wedding planners, designers, and print vendors.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

To become India's most trusted digital wedding platform by simplifying invitation design, event planning, guest communication, and wedding management through modern technology and AI.

---

# 3. Mission

Enable every couple to create premium wedding experiences without requiring technical or design expertise.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

The current wedding invitation process presents several challenges:

- Hiring designers is expensive.
- Revisions consume time.
- Printing requires multiple iterations.
- RSVP tracking is manual.
- Guest management relies on spreadsheets.
- Sharing invitations across platforms is inconsistent.
- Event information becomes fragmented.

The result is unnecessary stress during wedding planning.

# 15. Success Metrics

The product will be considered successful if:

- Customers can create invitations within 15 minutes.
- Less than 2% of exports fail.
- Average page load remains below 2 seconds.
- Customer satisfaction exceeds 90%.
- Monthly uptime remains above 99.9%.

---

# 16. Product Principles

- Easy to learn
- Fast to use
- Mobile-friendly
- Accessible
- Secure
- Beautiful by default
- Performance-oriented
- AI-assisted where valuable

---

# 17. Out of Scope (MVP)

The following will not be included in the first release:

- Native Android application
- Native iOS application
- Live video streaming
- Gift registry
- Vendor bidding
- AI voice assistant
- Multi-event family dashboards

These features may be considered in later phases based on customer feedback.

---

# 18. Risks

- Users may prefer traditional designers.
- AI-generated designs may require refinement.
- Seasonal demand may affect revenue.
- Template quality must remain consistently high.
- Printing partners require operational management.

Mitigation strategies include continuous user testing, premium template curation, diversified revenue streams, and strategic partnerships.

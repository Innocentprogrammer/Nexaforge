# Sales Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the sales strategy, customer acquisition framework, pricing methodology, sales process, customer onboarding, renewal strategy, and revenue operations for NexaForge Technologies.

The objective is to build a predictable, scalable, and customer-focused revenue engine.

---

# Sales Vision

> Become the preferred technology partner for startups, SMEs, and growing businesses by delivering measurable business outcomes rather than simply selling software.

---

# Revenue Philosophy

Revenue should come from long-term relationships.

The objective is not to maximize one-time sales but to maximize:

- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Success
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Customer Retention

# Common Customer Objections

## "Too expensive."

Response

Focus on ROI, automation benefits, and long-term cost savings.

---

## "We already use another solution."

Response

Discuss migration support and unique value, avoiding negative comparisons.

---

## "We'll decide later."

Response

Understand the reason for delay and schedule a follow-up with a clear next step.

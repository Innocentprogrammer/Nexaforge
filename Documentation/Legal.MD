# Legal & Compliance Manual

## Purpose

This document establishes the legal, regulatory, contractual, and governance framework for NexaForge Technologies.

It defines:

- Company registration
- Intellectual Property (IP)
- Corporate governance
- Contracts
- Privacy & data protection
- Employment compliance
- Customer agreements
- Vendor agreements
- Risk management
- Compliance practices

This document should evolve with the company and applicable laws.

---

# Legal Vision

> Build a legally compliant, ethically governed, and globally trusted technology company.

Legal compliance is not just a requirement—it is a competitive advantage.

---

# Company Structure

### Recommended Entity (India)

Private Limited Company

Reason:

- Limited liability
- Investor friendly
- Better credibility
- ESOP support
- Easier fundraising

---

# Registration Checklist

Complete before commercial launch:

- Company Incorporation
- PAN
- TAN
- GST Registration (if applicable)
- Professional Tax (state-specific)
- Shops & Establishments registration (if applicable)
- MSME/Udyam Registration (recommended)
- Bank Current Account
- Trademark Application

---

# Intellectual Property (IP)

Protect all original assets.

Includes:

- Company name
- Logo
- Product names
- Source code
- UI/UX designs
- Documentation
- Marketing assets
- Databases
- AI models (where applicable)

Ownership should remain with NexaForge unless explicitly transferred by contract.

---

# Trademark Strategy

Register:

- NexaForge
- NexaInvite
- Future product names
- Company logo
- Tagline (if distinctive)

Review trademark availability before filing in each jurisdiction.

---

# Copyright

Automatically applies to:

- Source code
- Documentation
- Website content
- Images created by the company
- Marketing material

Maintain clear records of authorship and ownership.

---

# Patent Policy

Consider patent filings only when:

- A solution is novel.
- It provides a meaningful competitive advantage.
- Commercial value justifies the cost.

Not every innovation requires a patent.

---

# Corporate Governance

Board responsibilities:

- Strategic oversight
- Financial accountability
- Risk management
- Ethical leadership

Founders should maintain clear separation between personal and company finances.

---

# Legal Agreements

The following agreements should be standardized and reviewed by legal counsel:

- Customer Service Agreement
- Software Development Agreement
- SaaS Subscription Agreement
- NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)
- Employment Agreement
- Internship Agreement
- Vendor Agreement
- Partnership Agreement
- Consultant Agreement

---

# Customer Terms of Service

Every product should publish Terms of Service covering:

- Acceptable use
- User responsibilities
- Payments
- Refunds
- Service availability
- Suspension or termination
- Limitation of liability
- Governing law

---

# Privacy Policy

The privacy policy should explain:

- Data collected
- Purpose of collection
- Storage methods
- Data sharing practices
- User rights
- Cookie usage
- Contact information

Collect only the information necessary to deliver the service.

---

# Cookie Policy

If cookies or similar technologies are used:

- Explain their purpose.
- Allow users to manage preferences where required.
- Distinguish between essential and optional cookies.

---

# Data Protection

Key principles:

- Minimize personal data collection.
- Encrypt sensitive information.
- Restrict access based on role.
- Log administrative actions.
- Back up critical data.
- Define retention schedules.

---

# Information Security

Minimum controls:

- HTTPS everywhere
- Multi-factor authentication for administrators
- Secure password hashing
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Regular vulnerability assessments
- Security monitoring

---

# Regulatory Compliance

Depending on operations and customer locations, monitor obligations under:

- Indian IT regulations
- Consumer protection laws
- Tax regulations
- International privacy frameworks (for global expansion)

Compliance requirements should be reviewed periodically as the business grows.

---

# Employment Compliance

Maintain:

- Employment contracts
- Leave policy
- Working hours
- Payroll records
- Statutory deductions
- Workplace conduct policies

Follow applicable labor laws in each operating jurisdiction.

---

# Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA)

Require NDAs where appropriate for:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Vendors
- Strategic partners

Confidential information should be clearly defined.

---

# Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation

Where legally appropriate and enforceable:

- Protect legitimate business interests.
- Avoid unreasonable restrictions.
- Seek legal review before implementation.

---

# Vendor Compliance

Before onboarding vendors:

- Verify identity
- Review pricing
- Assess security practices (where relevant)
- Execute written agreements
- Define service-level expectations

---

# Payment Compliance

Use licensed payment providers.

Do not store:

- Card numbers
- CVV values
- Sensitive payment credentials

Rely on tokenized payment solutions where possible.

---

# Refund Policy

Publish a transparent refund policy.

Include:

- Eligibility
- Processing timelines
- Non-refundable items
- Subscription cancellation rules

Consistency builds customer trust.

---

# Software Licensing

Track all third-party software.

Maintain records of:

- License type
- Expiration
- Renewal dates
- Usage restrictions

Avoid unlicensed software.

---

# Open Source Policy

Before using open-source software:

- Review the license.
- Verify compatibility.
- Document dependencies.
- Contribute improvements where appropriate.

Maintain a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) for production systems.

---

# Record Retention

Maintain records for:

- Financial documents
- Contracts
- Employment records
- Audit reports
- Customer agreements
- Tax filings

Retention periods should align with applicable legal requirements.

---

# Risk Management

Potential legal risks:

- Intellectual property disputes
- Contract disputes
- Data breaches
- Regulatory changes
- Employment disputes

Mitigation:

- Written agreements
- Legal reviews
- Security controls
- Insurance where appropriate

---

# Insurance (Future)

Consider:

- Professional indemnity insurance
- Cybersecurity insurance
- Directors & Officers (D&O) insurance
- General liability insurance

Insurance needs should be reviewed annually.

---

# Compliance Reviews

Recommended frequency:

- Quarterly internal review
- Annual external legal review

Document findings and corrective actions.

---

# Corporate Policies

Maintain separate documents for:

- Code of Conduct
- Information Security Policy
- HR Policy
- Remote Work Policy
- Procurement Policy
- Incident Response Plan
- Business Continuity Plan

---

# Ethics

NexaForge commits to:

- Honest business practices
- Respect for customers
- Fair competition
- Responsible technology
- Transparency
- Accountability

Ethics should guide every business decision.

---

# Business Continuity

Prepare plans for:

- Cloud outages
- Cyber incidents
- Key personnel loss
- Natural disasters
- Vendor failures

Critical services should have documented recovery procedures.

# International Expansion

Before entering new markets:

- Review local laws.
- Assess tax implications.
- Localize contracts.
- Review privacy obligations.
- Register trademarks where appropriate.

# Legal KPIs

Track:

- Contract turnaround time
- Trademark status
- Compliance audit completion
- Security incidents
- Policy review completion
- Vendor agreement coverage

# Founder's Legal Principles

The founders commit to:

- Respecting intellectual property.
- Protecting customer data.
- Maintaining transparent contracts.
- Operating ethically.
- Seeking professional legal advice for significant matters.

# Success Indicators

The legal framework is successful when:

- Contracts are standardized.
- Intellectual property is protected.
- Regulatory obligations are met.
- Customer trust remains high.
- Legal disputes are minimized through proactive governance.

A strong legal foundation protects the company's assets, supports sustainable growth, and builds confidence among customers, employees, partners, and investors.

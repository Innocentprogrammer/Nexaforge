# Logo Guidelines

## Purpose

This document defines the official logo system for **NexaForge Technologies**.

A logo is more than a graphic—it represents the company's values, mission, engineering excellence, and long-term vision.

These guidelines ensure that every use of the NexaForge identity remains consistent across digital, print, merchandise, presentations, social media, and software products.

---

# Brand Philosophy

The NexaForge logo should communicate:

- Innovation
- Engineering Excellence
- Trust
- Precision
- Scalability
- Intelligence
- Simplicity
- Future Technology

The logo should immediately feel like it belongs to a modern global technology company.

---

**Nexa**

# Logo Style

Preferred style:

- Flat Design
- Geometric
- Minimal
- Modern
- Timeless

Avoid:

- Complex gradients
- 3D effects
- Heavy shadows
- Clipart
- Cartoon styles
- Skeuomorphic elements

The logo should be based on simple geometric shapes.

- Circles

# Minimum Size

Digital

Height:

24 px

Print

Height:

15 mm

Smaller sizes should use the icon-only version.

---

# Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter **N**.

No text, images, or graphics should enter this area.

---

# Background Usage

Preferred:

- White
- Deep Navy
- Light Gray

Avoid:

- Busy backgrounds
- Low-contrast photography
- Patterns
- Neon colors

---

# Incorrect Usage

Never:

- Stretch
- Compress
- Rotate
- Skew
- Change colors
- Add outlines
- Add shadows
- Add gradients
- Rearrange elements
- Use unofficial fonts

Consistency is mandatory.

# Future Evolution

The logo should remain stable for at least five years.

Minor refinements are acceptable.

Complete redesigns should occur only if:

- Company strategy changes significantly
- Global rebranding is required
- The logo no longer reflects the company's identity

Brand recognition is built through consistency.

---

# Conclusion

The NexaForge logo is the visual foundation of the company's identity.

It represents innovation, engineering precision, and long-term trust.

Every employee, designer, partner, and vendor is responsible for preserving the integrity of the brand by following these guidelines consistently across all touchpoints.

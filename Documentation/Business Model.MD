# Business Model

## Purpose

This document defines how NexaForge Technologies creates value, delivers value, and captures value.

A successful company is not built around products alone.

It is built around a sustainable business model that consistently generates revenue while solving meaningful customer problems.

The objective of this document is to define the company's commercial strategy for the next ten years.

---

# Business Philosophy

NexaForge Technologies follows a hybrid business model consisting of:

• Service Business

• SaaS Business

• Consulting Business

• Product Licensing

• Subscription Revenue

• AI Solutions

• Managed Services

• Marketplace Revenue (Future)

Unlike traditional software agencies that depend entirely on client projects, NexaForge will build recurring revenue through proprietary software products.

---

# Why Hybrid Model?

A startup generally faces two problems:

## Problem 1

Product companies often spend years developing software before generating revenue.

Result:

Cash Flow Problems

---

## Problem 2

Service companies generate revenue but struggle to scale because income depends on billable hours.

Result:

Limited Growth

---

## NexaForge Solution

Generate immediate revenue through services.

Invest profits into SaaS products.

Allow SaaS subscriptions to eventually become the company's primary income source.

This creates predictable recurring revenue while maintaining healthy cash flow.

# Closing Statement

The business model of NexaForge Technologies is intentionally designed to balance short-term sustainability with long-term scalability.

Services provide the financial engine for growth, while SaaS products create recurring revenue and long-term enterprise value.

Our success will not be measured by the number of projects delivered but by the number of businesses that grow because of the technology we build.

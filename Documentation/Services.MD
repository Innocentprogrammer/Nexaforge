# Service Portfolio

## Purpose

This document defines the complete service portfolio offered by NexaForge Technologies.

Unlike a traditional software agency that focuses only on website development or application development, NexaForge aims to become a long-term technology partner by offering end-to-end digital transformation services.

Every service should contribute to one or more of the following objectives:

- Generate immediate revenue.
- Build long-term client relationships.
- Create opportunities to sell NexaForge SaaS products.
- Strengthen the company's brand and market presence.

---

# Service Philosophy

We do not sell technology.

We solve business problems.

Every engagement should begin by understanding the client's business objectives before recommending any technology solution.

Our services must always be:

# Cross-Selling Strategy

Every client engagement should identify opportunities to introduce complementary products and services.

Examples:

- Website client → NexaCRM
- NexaCRM client → NexaFlow
- NexaInvite customer → Printing Partner Network
- ERP client → Power BI dashboards
- QA client → CI/CD implementation

The objective is to become the customer's long-term technology partner rather than a one-time vendor.

# Introduction

NexaForge Technologies is a modern technology company dedicated to helping businesses embrace digital transformation through innovative software products, enterprise-grade services, artificial intelligence, automation, and cloud solutions.

Unlike traditional software agencies that focus solely on website development, NexaForge aims to become a long-term technology partner for businesses by offering scalable products, recurring Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platforms, consulting, automation, and managed technology services.

The company is designed with a product-first mindset while maintaining a strong service division to generate sustainable revenue during the early stages of growth.

# Flagship Product

## NexaInvite

NexaInvite is an AI-powered wedding invitation and event management platform that allows users to:

- Choose professional templates
- Customize designs
- Generate AI-assisted invitations
- Manage guest lists
- Track RSVP responses
- Create wedding websites
- Share invitations digitally
- Download high-quality printable cards

NexaInvite serves as the company's first SaaS product and establishes the foundation for future event management and digital publishing solutions.

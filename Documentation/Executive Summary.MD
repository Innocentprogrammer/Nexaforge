# Company Overview

NexaForge Technologies is a technology company focused on helping startups, small businesses, and enterprises adopt digital solutions that improve efficiency, customer engagement, and operational scalability.

The company combines a service-based business model with scalable Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) products to create both immediate revenue and long-term recurring income.

The initial flagship product, **NexaInvite**, will be an AI-powered invitation and event management platform. Over time, the company will expand into customer relationship management, enterprise resource planning, workflow automation, AI solutions, cloud services, and business software.

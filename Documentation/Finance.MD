# Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Includes:

- Cloud infrastructure
- Payment gateway fees
- SMS/Email charges
- Storage
- CDN usage

Target:

Maintain Gross Margin above **70%** for SaaS offerings.

---

# Gross Margin Targets

| Business Unit | Target Margin |
|---------------|--------------:|
| SaaS | 75–85% |
| IT Services | 35–50% |
| Enterprise Projects | 40–60% |

---

# Break-Even Analysis

Primary Objective:

Achieve monthly break-even by ensuring recurring revenue covers fixed operating expenses.

Key drivers:

- Consistent client acquisition
- Subscription renewals
- Controlled operational costs

---

# Cash Flow Strategy

Priority Order:

1. Operational Expenses
2. Employee Salaries
3. Taxes & Compliance
4. Product Development
5. Marketing
6. Expansion

Maintain a minimum cash reserve equivalent to **6 months** of operating expenses.

# Risk Management

Potential Risks:

- Slow customer acquisition
- Higher infrastructure costs
- Payment defaults
- Regulatory changes
- Competitive pricing pressure

Mitigation:

- Diversified revenue streams
- Lean operations
- Emergency reserve fund
- Quarterly financial reviews

---

# Tax & Compliance

Maintain:

- GST registration
- Income tax filings
- TDS compliance
- Annual financial audits
- Statutory records

Consult a qualified Chartered Accountant for ongoing compliance.

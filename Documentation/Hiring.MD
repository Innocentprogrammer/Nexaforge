# Hiring Guide

## Purpose

This document defines the hiring philosophy, recruitment process, organizational structure, onboarding, performance management, career growth, and employee development framework for NexaForge Technologies.

People are the company's greatest competitive advantage. Hiring decisions should prioritize long-term potential, technical excellence, integrity, and cultural alignment.

---

# Hiring Vision

> Build a world-class engineering and business team that creates exceptional products while fostering a culture of ownership, continuous learning, and customer success.

---

# Core Hiring Principles

Every hiring decision should reflect the following principles:

- Hire for attitude and learning ability.
- Prioritize integrity over experience.
- Seek problem solvers rather than task executors.
- Encourage diversity of thought and background.
- Build teams that collaborate effectively.
- Invest in long-term employee growth.

# Interview Process

## Stage 1 – Resume Screening

Evaluate:

- Skills
- Relevant projects
- Experience
- Communication
- Learning mindset

---

## Stage 2 – Technical Assessment

Assess:

- Problem-solving
- Coding ability
- System design (for senior roles)
- Debugging
- Best practices

---

## Stage 3 – Technical Interview

Topics may include:

- Programming
- Databases
- APIs
- Testing
- Security
- Architecture
- Project discussion

Interviewers should encourage candidates to explain their reasoning.

---

## Stage 4 – Behavioral Interview

Evaluate:

- Communication
- Teamwork
- Ownership
- Conflict resolution
- Adaptability
- Customer focus

---

## Stage 5 – HR Interview

Discuss:

- Compensation
- Benefits
- Notice period
- Policies
- Career aspirations

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Candidates should be evaluated on:

| Category | Weight |
|----------|--------:|
| Technical Skills | 40% |
| Problem Solving | 20% |
| Communication | 15% |
| Cultural Fit | 15% |
| Learning Ability | 10% |

# Onboarding Process

Day 1

- Welcome session
- HR documentation
- Laptop and accounts
- Company introduction

Week 1

- Product overview
- Team introductions
- Development environment setup
- Initial training

Month 1

- Assigned mentor
- First project
- Regular check-ins

Objective:

Enable new hires to become productive as quickly as possible.

# Founder's Hiring Philosophy

The founder believes:

- Great companies are built by great people.

# Conclusion

Hiring at NexaForge is a strategic investment rather than an operational task.

By recruiting talented individuals, supporting continuous growth, and maintaining a culture of ownership and innovation, NexaForge will build the people foundation required to achieve its long-term vision of becoming a globally respected technology company.
